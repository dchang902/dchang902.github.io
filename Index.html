Elden ring

Explore, be thorough and do any dungeons you find. Keep track of what NPCs you talk to and what they want you to do. Pick up anything you find. Level up weapons when you can. Don't skimp on vigor, it'll make your playthrough a lot easier.

Order you should play

Limgrave, then Weeping Peninsula, then Lakes, then Altus Plateau and the Capital.
